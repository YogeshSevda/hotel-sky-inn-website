Sikar Garh Fort

Madho Niwas Kothi

A 19th-century royal mansion in Sikar, now a heritage museum offering a glimpse of local royal life.

Devgarh Fort

An 18th-century hilltop fort just outside the city, with palaces, temples and reservoirs to explore.

Harshnath Temple

An ancient Shiva temple set on a hill in the Aravalli range, just outside the city.

Khatu Shyam Ji

Jeen Mata Mandir

Nawalgarh

Fatehpur

~70 km

Mandawa

The most photographed of the Shekhawati towns — an easy full-day trip from Sikar.
